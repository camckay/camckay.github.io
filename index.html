rclone (personal use)

This is a private, personal-use configuration of the open-source command-line tool rclone. It is used by its owner to manage and back up files in their own Google Drive, and the owner's family member's Google Drive, from a computer the owner controls.

This application is not offered to the public and has no website, accounts, or hosted service. It does not collect, store or share user data on any server. Files are transferred only between Google Drive and the owner's own computers.
