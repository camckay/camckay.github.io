Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This application is a private, personal-use configuration of the open-source tool rclone. It is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

When the owner authorizes the application, it can access files in the authorizing Google account's Google Drive. This access is used only to list, copy, back up, and remove duplicate files in that account's own Drive.

How data is used and stored

Data is transferred directly between Google Drive and computers controlled by the owner. No Google user data is sent to or stored on any server operated by the developer, and none is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Authorization tokens are stored only in the owner's local rclone configuration.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.
